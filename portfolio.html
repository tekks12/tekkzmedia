The brief

Fitven sells sportswear, exercise equipment and gym accessories from two stores. They needed a place where customers could browse the full range online and order without the friction of a checkout.

What we built

  • A product catalogue split into clear categories, from spikes and boots to martial arts and strength gear
  • "No cart, just chat": every product goes straight to a WhatsApp order
  • Both store locations with opening hours, so customers know where to collect
  • A trust section and a quick request form for items not listed

The brief

Wired Solutions offers a wide range of technology services, from IT sales and structured cabling to CCTV, Wi-Fi, PABX and device repairs. They needed a site that makes all of that easy to understand and easy to act on.

What we built

  • Services organised into four clear areas, so visitors find what they need fast
  • A dedicated repairs section for walk-in device fixes
  • A simple four-step process, from consultation to ongoing support
  • A quote request form and WhatsApp contact built into the page
